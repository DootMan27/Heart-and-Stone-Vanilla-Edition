# Heart & Stone: Vanilla Edition

A vanilla-friendly overhaul of equipment progression. Better durability, cleaner tooltips, and a reworked early game. No new items, mobs, or blocks.

**Installing this will convert all your existing gear to the pack's stats permanently.** Back up your world first.

## Features

- **Tools & armor last way longer** - most items get 1.5x-6x their vanilla durability, so you don't need Mending as much.
- **Stone tools are no longer craftable** - Copper tools take their place as the new early tier.
- **Wooden tools got buffed to compensate** - the wooden pickaxe can even mine copper ore now.
- **Cleaner tooltips** - with simple icons instead of vanilla's clutter.
- **Gold and leather armor are actually worth wearing now** - gold reduces burning time, leather reduces fall damage.
- **Gold tools also got a buff** - they keep their naturally fast mining speed, attack noticeably faster than before, and no longer break after a few uses.
- **Netherite hits much harder** - stats reflect how much of a grind it is to get.

## Installing

Drop the pack into your world's `datapacks` folder and run `/reload` (or restart the world).

**Note:** Removing the pack later won't undo the conversion - items keep their new stats either way.

*For Minecraft 1.21.11 - 26.2*
